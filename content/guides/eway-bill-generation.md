Generating an e-way bill takes a few minutes once your invoice is ready, but small slips — a wrong PIN code, the wrong sub-type, a missing vehicle number — cause most of the trouble later. This guide walks through the official portal flow field by field, then covers the other generation routes and how the E-Way Easy app workflow fits in.

## Before you start

Keep these ready:

- The tax invoice, bill of supply or delivery challan for the goods.
- Recipient GSTIN (or "URP" for an unregistered recipient), name and address.
- Dispatch-from and ship-to PIN codes.
- HSN code, quantity, unit and taxable value of each item, with tax rates.
- Transporter ID (if a transporter is involved) and vehicle number, or the transport document number for rail, air or ship.
- Access to the mobile number registered for OTP-based login.

The full list, split into Part A and Part B, is in [Documents and Data Required](/documents-required-for-eway-bill).

> **Note:** As reported by GSTN advisories, from 1 January 2025 an e-way bill cannot be generated against a base document dated more than 180 days before the generation date. Generate promptly.

## Step-by-step on the official portal

### Step 1: Log in

Go to the [official e-way bill portal](https://ewaybillgst.gov.in/) (or the [E-Way Bill 2.0 portal](https://ewaybill2.gst.gov.in/), which works with synchronised data). Enter your username, password and captcha. Multi-factor authentication now applies to all taxpayers, so you will be asked for an OTP on the registered mobile number or an authenticator.

First-time users register on the portal with their GSTIN and the mobile number registered with GST.

### Step 2: Choose Generate New

From the e-way bill menu on the dashboard, choose the option to generate a new e-way bill. The Part A form opens.

### Step 3: Select transaction type

- **Outward** — you are the supplier sending goods out.
- **Inward** — you are the recipient bringing goods in (for example, when buying from an unregistered supplier).

The portal also asks for the transaction pattern: a regular movement, or a "bill to–ship to" / "bill from–dispatch from" arrangement where the billing and delivery parties or places differ. Pick the one that matches your invoice.

### Step 4: Select sub-type

Sub-type is the reason for movement. Common options:

| Sub-type | When to use |
|---|---|
| Supply | Normal sale |
| Export / Import | Cross-border movement |
| Job Work / Job Work Returns | Goods sent to or returned from a job worker |
| SKD/CKD/Lots | Machinery sent in parts or lots |
| Recipient Not Known | Line sales where the buyer is not fixed at dispatch |
| For Own Use | Stock transfer or own goods moved |
| Exhibition or Fairs | Goods taken to a trade fair |
| Line Sales | Goods sent to sell along a route |
| Sales Return | Goods coming back from the buyer |
| Others | Movement not covered above (describe it) |

### Step 5: Enter document details

Choose the document type (tax invoice, bill of supply, delivery challan, bill of entry or others), then enter the document number and date exactly as on the paper or PDF document. The combination of GSTIN, document type and document number must be unique — the portal will not accept the same document twice.

### Step 6: Fill the rest of Part A

- **From:** supplier name, GSTIN, dispatch address and PIN.
- **To:** recipient name, GSTIN (or URP), ship-to address and PIN, state.
- **Item details:** product name, description, HSN code, quantity, unit, taxable value, and tax rates (CGST + SGST for intra-state, IGST for inter-state, plus cess if any).
- **Totals:** the portal adds up the consignment value. Check it against your invoice.
- **Transporter details:** Transporter ID and name, if a transporter is involved.
- **Approximate distance:** the portal suggests the PIN-to-PIN distance. You can enter up to 10% more if the actual route is longer. See [Distance Rules](/eway-bill-distance-rules).

### Step 7: Fill Part B

Select the mode of transport:

- **Road:** enter the vehicle number (format like GJ03AB1234), vehicle type (regular or over-dimensional cargo).
- **Rail, air or ship:** enter the transport document number (RR, airway bill, bill of lading) and date.

If the transporter will add the vehicle later, you can leave Part B blank and enter the Transporter ID in Part A. The transporter then updates Part B from their login before the goods move.

### Step 8: Submit

Click submit. The portal validates the data. If something is wrong, it shows a message near the field or at the top. Fix it and submit again. On success, you get the e-way bill with a 12-digit EWB number, generation date and time, and validity.

### Step 9: Print or share

Print the e-way bill or share the number with the driver. The person in charge of the vehicle must carry the invoice or challan and the e-way bill (printed, as the number, or via a mapped RFID device).

## Other ways to generate

The official system supports several routes. The exact steps for each are in the [user documentation](https://docs.ewaybillgst.gov.in/).

| Route | Who it suits | How it works in general |
|---|---|---|
| Web portal | Most businesses | Manual entry as described above |
| SMS | Users who need to generate from a basic phone | Register the mobile number on the portal, then send coded SMS messages in the prescribed format |
| Official mobile app | Taxpayers and transporters on the move | Register the device on the portal, then use the official app |
| Bulk upload | Businesses with many invoices a day | Prepare a JSON file with the official Excel-based bulk generation tool and upload it — see [Bulk Generation](/bulk-eway-bill-generation) |
| API / GSP integration | Large taxpayers with ERP systems | Authorised integration sends data directly to the e-way bill system |
| Using IRN | Taxpayers issuing e-invoices | Part A is filled from the e-invoice; you add transport details |

## How the E-Way Easy app workflow fits in

E-Way Easy is an independent Android app, not a government portal. It is designed around one invoice at a time, in three steps:

1. **Invoice** — Your seller details are saved. Enter the buyer's GSTIN and the name, address, state and PIN fill automatically (leave it empty for an unregistered buyer). Pick products from your saved list with search; the HSN code fills from the product, and you can edit it. Invoice numbers follow your own series.
2. **Review EWB** — The e-way bill details are laid out from the invoice. The distance is calculated automatically from the PIN codes, and you can edit it manually. Check the values before you move on.
3. **Generated** — The sales invoice and e-way bill details are completed in the same flow, with an invoice preview before saving.

The app's dashboard shows your sales for the financial year, invoice and e-way bill counts, and recent activity. The same rules — consignment value, distance margin, Part B — apply whichever tool you use. See [features](/features) or [download the app](/download).

## Example (illustrative)

A Rajkot pipe manufacturer (GSTIN `24ABCDE1234F1Z5`) sells PVC pipes worth ₹1,20,000 plus 18% GST to a builder in Ahmedabad (GSTIN `24PQRSX5678K1Z2`).

| Field | Entry |
|---|---|
| Transaction type | Outward, regular |
| Sub-type | Supply |
| Document | Tax invoice RPM/26-27/0412, 14-10-2026 |
| HSN | Code from the product master |
| Taxable value | ₹1,20,000 |
| CGST 9% + SGST 9% | ₹10,800 + ₹10,800 |
| Consignment value | ₹1,41,600 |
| From PIN → To PIN | 360xxx → 380xxx |
| Distance | About 220 km (portal suggestion; up to 10% more allowed) |
| Part B | Road, vehicle GJ03AB1234 |

Because the distance is between 201 and 400 km, validity is 2 days.

## Common mistakes

- Choosing "Supply" for a stock transfer to your own branch — use the sub-type that matches the actual reason.
- Entering IGST for an intra-state movement, or CGST/SGST for inter-state.
- Typing a different invoice number from the one printed on the invoice.
- Leaving Part B blank and forgetting to tell the transporter to fill it.
- Entering a distance far above the portal suggestion and getting rejected.

## Limitations

Some situations — imports with bill of entry, SKD/CKD lots, consolidated e-way bills for many consignments in one vehicle — have extra steps. Check the official user documentation for those.

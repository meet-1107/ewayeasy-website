Cancellation is the only real undo button in the e-way bill system, and it works for just 24 hours. Use it when the goods will not move, or when Part A details are wrong. Miss the window and your options shrink quickly.

## The rule in short

Under Rule 138(9) of the CGST Rules:

- An e-way bill can be cancelled **within 24 hours of generation**,
- where goods are **not transported**, or are **not transported as per the details** in the e-way bill,
- but it **cannot** be cancelled if it has been **verified in transit** by an officer.

## When you should cancel

| Situation | Cancel? | Then |
|---|---|---|
| Order called off before dispatch | Yes | Nothing more, unless the invoice also needs a credit note |
| Wrong recipient GSTIN | Yes | Generate a fresh e-way bill with correct details |
| Wrong HSN, quantity or value | Yes | Generate afresh |
| Wrong ship-to PIN or distance | Yes | Generate afresh |
| Duplicate e-way bill generated by mistake | Yes, cancel the extra one | Keep the correct one |
| Only the vehicle number is wrong | No | Update Part B instead — see [Correction](/eway-bill-correction) |
| Change of transporter | No | Update the Transporter ID |
| Goods already moving and checked by an officer | Not allowed | Seek professional advice if details are wrong |

## How to cancel on the portal

1. Log in to the [official e-way bill portal](https://ewaybillgst.gov.in/) with your username, password and OTP.
2. Open the e-way bill menu and choose the cancel option.
3. Enter the 12-digit e-way bill number.
4. Confirm the bill details shown.
5. Select a reason — commonly duplicate, order cancelled, data entry mistake, or others — and add remarks.
6. Submit. The status changes to cancelled.

The e-way bill can also be cancelled through other official modes such as SMS or API, for users set up for them. See the [user documentation](https://docs.ewaybillgst.gov.in/).

## Example (illustrative)

A Coimbatore pump maker generates an e-way bill on **14-10-2026 at 11:20** for a Bengaluru dealer. At 15:00, the accounts team notices the dealer's GSTIN was copied from an old branch.

- Cancellation allowed until **15-10-2026, 11:20**.
- The team cancels at 15:10 with reason "Data entry mistake", then generates a new e-way bill with the correct GSTIN.
- The driver is given the new e-way bill number; the old printout is destroyed so there is no confusion at a checkpoint.

## Who can cancel and who cannot

| Person | Can cancel? |
|---|---|
| The person who generated the e-way bill (supplier, recipient or transporter) | Yes, within 24 hours |
| A transporter who was only assigned the bill to fill Part B | No — ask the generator |
| The recipient who did not generate it | No — but can reject it within the acceptance window |
| An officer | Not applicable; a bill verified by an officer cannot be cancelled by anyone |

If your transporter generated the e-way bill on your behalf, the cancellation must also be done from the transporter's login. Agree in advance who will handle cancellations so that no one waits for the other while the 24 hours run out.

## Special cases

### E-way bill with Part A only

A bill generated with only Part A (no vehicle yet) can be cancelled in the same way within 24 hours. If the goods are not going to move at all, cancel it rather than leaving an unused bill on record.

### E-way bill generated from an IRN

For e-invoice taxpayers, the e-way bill generated using the IRN is a separate record from the e-invoice. Cancelling the e-way bill does not cancel the IRN, and cancelling the IRN has its own rules and time limit on the Invoice Registration Portal. If both the sale and the movement are off, handle each one in its own system.

### Consolidated e-way bill

A consolidated e-way bill (Form GST EWB-02) groups several individual e-way bills in one vehicle. If one consignment is pulled out of the vehicle, the individual bills and the consolidated bill need to be handled as per the portal's options — usually by regenerating the consolidated bill for the remaining consignments. Check the user documentation for the current steps.

### Bulk-generated bills

If a bulk upload created a wrong or duplicate e-way bill, cancel those specific bills within 24 hours. Do not re-upload the whole file to "replace" them. See [Bulk Generation](/bulk-eway-bill-generation).

## What to record when you cancel

Keep a short note for each cancellation in your e-way bill register:

| Column | Example (illustrative) |
|---|---|
| Original EWB number | 3310 xxxx xxxx |
| Invoice number | CPM/26-27/1187 |
| Generated at | 14-10-2026 11:20 |
| Cancelled at | 14-10-2026 15:10 |
| Reason selected | Data entry mistake |
| Remark | Old branch GSTIN of buyer used |
| Replacement EWB | 3310 yyyy yyyy |

This takes a minute and saves a lot of explanation later, especially when e-way bill data is compared with GSTR-1.

## After the 24-hour window

The portal will not let you cancel. What remains:

- **Recipient rejection.** The recipient can reject the e-way bill within 72 hours of it being made available, or before delivery if earlier. Ask the buyer to reject if the goods were never sent to them.
- **Keep evidence.** If goods never moved, keep records — the reason, internal notes, the transporter's confirmation that no vehicle was dispatched — in case the unused e-way bill is questioned later.
- **Generate correctly next time.** If the goods are still to move, a new e-way bill is needed with correct details.
- **Watch return reconciliation.** E-way bill data is compared with returns. An uncancelled bill for a sale that did not happen can raise questions.

## Cancelling the e-way bill vs cancelling the invoice

These are separate. Cancelling an e-way bill only cancels the movement record. If the sale itself is off:

- Before reporting in returns, follow your accounting process for cancelling the invoice.
- After reporting, a credit note is the usual route.
- For e-invoices, the IRN has its own cancellation rules and time limit on the IRP; check them separately.

## Common mistakes

- Cancelling for a wrong vehicle number, when a Part B update would do.
- Waiting until the next working day and missing the 24-hour limit.
- Cancelling the bill but letting the truck leave with the old printout.
- Assuming the invoice is also cancelled.
- Cancelling the correct bill instead of the duplicate.

## Tips

- Review every e-way bill as soon as it is generated, not at day's end.
- In the E-Way Easy app, the **Review EWB** step is the moment to check GSTIN, PIN, HSN and value before the details are completed — fixing an error there is much easier than cancelling later.
- Keep a simple register: EWB number, invoice number, time generated, status.

Cancelling an e-way bill sounds simple, and on the portal it mostly is. The hard part is the timing. The rule gives you a 24-hour window, and many businesses only discover a mistake after that window has closed. This article explains when cancellation is allowed, how to do it, and what your options are if you are too late.

## The rule in one paragraph

Under Rule 138(9) of the CGST Rules, 2017, where an e-way bill has been generated but goods are either not transported or not transported as per the details furnished, the e-way bill may be cancelled electronically on the portal **within 24 hours of generation**. An e-way bill that has been **verified in transit** under the inspection rules cannot be cancelled.

That gives you three conditions to check:

1. Has it been less than 24 hours since generation?
2. Is there a valid reason — goods not moved, or details wrong?
3. Has the e-way bill not yet been verified by an officer?

If all three are true, you can cancel.

## Common reasons to cancel

- The order was cancelled after the e-way bill was generated.
- A Part A detail is wrong — recipient GSTIN, invoice number, invoice date, value, quantity, HSN or place of delivery.
- A duplicate e-way bill was generated for the same consignment by mistake.
- The consignment was split or combined differently than planned and the invoice itself was revised.

Remember that a wrong **vehicle number** does not need cancellation. Part B can be updated during validity — see [how to update vehicle details](/blog/how-to-update-vehicle-details-in-eway-bill).

## How to cancel on the portal

The menu labels may differ slightly between the main portal and the E-Way Bill 2.0 portal, but the process is broadly:

1. Log in to the e-way bill portal with your credentials and multi-factor authentication.
2. Open the **e-way bill** menu and choose **Cancel**.
3. Enter the 12-digit e-way bill number and fetch it.
4. Check that it is the right e-way bill — compare the document number, date and recipient.
5. Select a **reason** for cancellation (for example duplicate, order cancelled, data entry mistake, or others) and add remarks.
6. Submit. The status changes to cancelled and the number cannot be used again.

Only the person who generated the e-way bill can cancel it.

## Example (illustrative): wrong invoice value

A Surat textile wholesaler raises Invoice ST/2026/0418 for ₹1,12,000 plus 5% GST and generates an e-way bill at 4:30 pm on 3 March. At 9:00 am the next morning, the accounts team notices the invoice should have been ₹1,21,000 — two digits were swapped.

| Check | Status |
|---|---|
| Time since generation | 16.5 hours — within 24 hours |
| Goods moved? | Not yet dispatched |
| Verified in transit? | No |

Steps the wholesaler takes:

1. Cancels the e-way bill with reason "data entry mistake" before 4:30 pm on 4 March.
2. Corrects the invoice in its books as per its own invoicing practice (or issues a fresh invoice, depending on how its accounting treats the error).
3. Generates a new e-way bill with the correct value: ₹1,21,000 + ₹6,050 GST = ₹1,27,050.
4. Dispatches the goods with the new e-way bill.

Had the accounts team noticed at 6:00 pm on 4 March, the 24-hour window would have closed, and cancellation would no longer be possible.

## What if you have missed the 24-hour window?

This is the uncomfortable part. After 24 hours, the generator cannot cancel the e-way bill on the portal. Practical options depend on the situation:

- **If the goods have not moved and will not move**, the e-way bill simply lapses after its validity. Keep internal records (cancelled order, credit note if any) to explain why no movement happened. Your tax consultant can advise on how to reflect this in returns.
- **If the recipient is registered**, the recipient can reject the e-way bill within 72 hours of the details being made available to them, or before delivery, whichever is earlier. If not rejected, it is deemed accepted.
- **If Part A details are wrong and goods must still move**, take professional advice before generating any second document for the same consignment. A mismatch between the invoice and the e-way bill during an inspection can create avoidable trouble.

> **Note:** GSTN reported that from 1 January 2025, e-way bills cannot be generated for documents dated more than 180 days before the generation date. If a correction drags on, check that your base document is still within that limit.

## Cancellation vs. rejection vs. update

| Action | Who | Time limit | Use for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cancel | Generator | 24 hours from generation | Goods not moved, Part A errors, duplicates |
| Reject | Recipient | 72 hours or delivery, whichever is earlier | Recipient did not order or does not accept the consignment as shown |
| Update Part B | Generator or assigned transporter | Any time during validity | Vehicle change, trans-shipment, first-time entry |
| Extend | Generator or transporter in charge | 8 hours before or after expiry | Goods delayed by exceptional circumstances |

## Common mistakes

- **Waiting for the end of the day** to review e-way bills. Errors found at the evening review may already be close to or beyond 24 hours if the e-way bill was generated the previous afternoon.
- **Cancelling after dispatch without stopping the vehicle.** If the goods are already on the road, cancelling leaves them moving without a valid e-way bill. Hold the vehicle, cancel, generate the new one, then move.
- **Cancelling the e-way bill but not fixing the invoice.** The new e-way bill must match a correct base document.
- **Cancelling for a vehicle number error.** That is a Part B update, not a cancellation.
- **Cancelling the wrong number.** In busy dispatch rooms with dozens of e-way bills, always match the document number before submitting.

## Reducing the need to cancel

Most cancellations come from data entry errors in Part A. A short checklist before generation helps — buyer GSTIN, invoice number and date, taxable value, tax amount, HSN and destination PIN. Our article on [avoiding errors in invoice and transport details](/blog/avoid-errors-in-invoice-and-transport-details) covers this in detail.

In the E-Way Easy app, buyer address, state and PIN fill automatically from the buyer's GSTIN, HSN fills from the saved product, and there is a dedicated Review EWB step before anything is generated. That review step is the right moment to catch the mistakes that would otherwise force a cancellation.

The app works out CGST + SGST or IGST, and you preview, save and download the PDF. Creating invoices is free; credits are used only if you go on to an e-way bill.

Most small traders already run their business from a phone. Orders arrive on WhatsApp and payments come by UPI, yet the invoice often waits for a computer or a bill book.

This guide shows how to make a GST invoice on an Android phone with the E-Way Easy app, and what to check before you send it. If you need one bill today and do not want to install anything, skip to the free online generator at the end.

## Before you start

To make a GST invoice on your phone, you need four things: an Android phone with the app, your business GSTIN, the buyer's GSTIN if they are registered, and your product names with HSN codes and GST rates. Gather the product details first, because they get saved once and reused on every invoice.

- An Android phone with the E-Way Easy app
- Your business GSTIN
- The buyer's GSTIN, if they are registered
- Your product names, HSN codes and GST rates

The app is for GST-registered traders, manufacturers, wholesalers and distributors. If you are not registered, you should not charge GST at all; read [invoice with GST vs without GST](/blog/invoice-with-gst-vs-without-gst) first.

If you also raise invoices in desktop software, give the phone its own prefix. Two tools pulling numbers from one series is the fastest way to get duplicate invoice numbers.

## Step-by-step: making the invoice

A GST invoice on the E-Way Easy app is built in the first stage of a three-stage flow: **1 Invoice → 2 Review EWB → 3 Generated**. You enter the buyer, products and quantities in stage 1 and preview the invoice. Stages 2 and 3 are used only when the goods need an e-way bill.

### Step 1: Add your business once

Sign up with email or Google and add your business GSTIN. Your seller details are saved and appear on every invoice. The dashboard then shows your business name and GSTIN, sales for the financial year, invoice and e-way bill counts, and recent activity.

### Step 2: Start a new invoice and enter the buyer

Open create invoice and type the buyer's GSTIN. Their **name, address, state and PIN fill automatically**. Check them against what the buyer told you; a mismatch is a sign the GSTIN may be wrong.

If the buyer is not registered, leave the GSTIN empty and enter their details by hand. Buyers are saved to your list, so a repeat customer is one tap next time.

If the goods go to a different address, fill **Ship To** as well. The tax type still follows the place of supply, which for a bill-to-ship-to sale of goods is generally the bill-to buyer's location.

### Step 3: Add products

Pick items from your saved products using search. The **HSN code fills from the product**, and you can edit it. Enter quantity and rate for each line; a new product is saved for future invoices.

Keep the product list accurate. A wrong rate saved once becomes a wrong rate on every invoice that uses it.

### Step 4: Let the tax split work itself out

The app compares the buyer's state with yours:

- **Same state:** CGST + SGST, each at half the rate
- **Different states:** IGST at the full rate

For the arithmetic behind this, including discounts and mixed rates, see [how to calculate GST on a sales invoice](/blog/how-to-calculate-gst-on-sales-invoice).

### Step 5: Check the invoice number

Invoice numbers follow your own series and are assigned automatically. Set the prefix to match your books so there are no gaps or duplicates in the financial year.

### Step 6: Preview

Preview the invoice before saving. This is where you catch the errors that matter: buyer GSTIN, HSN, quantity, rate, tax type and total. Invoices can also carry your bank details for payment.

### Step 7: Save and download the invoice

Save the invoice and download it as a PDF to send to the buyer, for example on WhatsApp or email. You do not need to generate an e-way bill to keep or send an invoice, and this part is free.

### Step 8 (if goods are moving): Continue to the e-way bill

If the consignment needs an e-way bill, continue to **Review EWB**. The **road distance between the two PIN codes fills automatically**, and you can edit it. Review, generate, and the e-way bill number appears on the **Generated** screen.

Bills are filed on the official e-way bill system through an authorised GSP, using your own portal API login, which you connect once in the app. See [e-way bill generation](/eway-bill-generation) for the rules behind each field.

## Already have the invoice as a PDF?

Import PDF lets you start from an invoice made elsewhere, such as your accounting software. The app reads the buyer, GSTIN, items, HSN codes and totals from the PDF. You then review and correct what it extracted before you generate, because invoice layouts vary and you are responsible for the final details.

Pay most attention to the GSTIN and the totals. A single misread digit in either will make the e-way bill fail or mismatch your books.

## Mistakes to avoid on mobile

Most mobile invoice mistakes come from trusting the small screen too much. Auto-fill saves typing but can carry a wrong GSTIN, an old rate or a stretched distance into the invoice. The checks below take under a minute and catch the errors that cause e-way bill rejections or a buyer losing input tax credit.

- **Accepting auto-filled details without reading them.** Check the buyer's name and state before you move on.
- **Fixing HSN or rate on one invoice only.** Update the saved product so the next invoice is right too.
- **Typing a guessed GSTIN for an unregistered buyer.** Leave the field empty instead.
- **Skipping the preview.** Scroll the full preview before saving.
- **Stretching the distance.** The portal allows up to 10% more than its own PIN-to-PIN distance, so keep edits within that.
- **Breaking your number series** by changing the prefix halfway through the year.
- **Charging GST when you should not.** Composition dealers and unregistered sellers do not charge GST.
